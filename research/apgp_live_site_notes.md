# Live APGP Website Notes

**Source reviewed:** <https://www.apgpaccommodation.com.au/> on 28 July 2026.

## Verified positioning

APGP presents itself as the **Accommodation Provider Growth Program**, an independent referral platform for SDA and SIL accommodation providers. Its stated purpose is to connect providers with a consistent, pre-qualified stream of NDIS participants, supporting occupancy, stability and provider growth.

## Verified service model

The website describes a results-based referral partnership: providers register free, describe available vacancies, and APGP sources, qualifies and matches participants. APGP states that it manages lead generation, intake, matching and support through to move-in. Its stated commercial model is **$0 upfront / pay only when a participant moves in**, with a success-based placement fee quoted per lead.

## Verified provider benefits

The live site identifies stable occupancy, less vacancy downtime, removal of marketing and recruitment overhead, stronger participant matching and more time for providers to deliver supports as core outcomes. It also advertises a live participant-enquiry feed for registered providers.

## Verified pathway sequence

The dedicated **How APGP Works** page presents the referral pathway in five steps:

1. **Register free and access the platform** — a provider account gives access to the live participant-enquiry feed and provider dashboard.
2. **Share vacancies or browse the live feed** — providers can describe available properties or select suitable enquiries.
3. **APGP handles the full intake process** — including discovery and qualification calls, suitability assessment, documentation collection, NDIS plan verification and property inspections.
4. **APGP supports the move-in** — through onboarding, tenancy setup and the first weeks of placement.
5. **Pay only after move-in** — the success fee is triggered only after completed intake and physical move-in.

The page states that the placement fee covers marketing and lead generation, discovery and qualification calls, suitability assessment, documentation collection, property matching, inspection scheduling and intake handover. It also describes ongoing support for vacancy management, matching and participant retention after placement.
